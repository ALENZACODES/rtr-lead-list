# First Lead List: Canada's Real-Time Rail Early Movers

Built Nov 12, 2026 in Toronto, Ontario.

## The seat I am playing
A go to market seat at Flinks, a Canadian company whose platform does bank connectivity, account verification and account to account payments (https://flinks.com). I picked it because it sits right where Canadian money movement is changing this month.

## Ideal customer in one sentence
Canadian digital first banks, fintechs, lenders and payment companies that move money between bank accounts for consumers or small businesses.

## Filters used
1. Headquartered and operating in Canada
2. Moves money in or out of customer bank accounts as a core part of the product (deposits, loan funding, payouts or pay by bank)
3. Digital first: customers open and fund accounts online
4. Excluded: the Big Six banks and US banks, which have their own in house teams; companies whose website I could not open

Pruned on the way: Bank of America (an at launch participant, but a US bank, so outside my ICP), plus four names whose websites would not load when I checked them.

## The signal and why it fits
Signal: **named as an at launch participant on Payments Canada's Real-Time Rail, Nov 9, 2026.**
A company that has just connected to 24/7 instant payments has a new reason to check account ownership before money moves, because instant payments cannot be pulled back the way a batch payment can. Payments Canada itself launched the rail with mandatory fraud services such as confirmation of payee, which tells me fraud and verification are top of mind for every participant.
Signal anatomy for each pick: what happened, who it happened to, when, and the original source.
